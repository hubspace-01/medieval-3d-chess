Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 10, 2026

Welcome to War Chess 3D (warchess3d.com). This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed when you play our browser-based 3D chess game, and what choices you have about it.

No account, no server-side profile

War Chess 3D runs entirely in your browser. It does not require registration, an email address, or any account, and we do not collect or store your name, postal address, phone number, or payment details on any server we operate.

What we store locally on your device

The game uses your browser's localStorage to remember your preferences between visits. Nothing in this list is transmitted to us — it stays on your device unless you clear it yourself:

  • kg.armies — the army skins you picked (Ivory Kingdom, Sun Empire, or Grande Armée) for each side.
  • kg.render — your graphics quality preset, brightness, and safe-rendering toggle.
  • kg.table — your camera seat-swing preference for two-player matches.
  • kg.cookie_consent — the choice you made in the cookie banner ("Accept All" or "Essential Only").

Device and performance signals

To pick a graphics preset your device can actually render smoothly, the game reads basic rendering capabilities in your browser — such as the GPU renderer string, device memory, and measured frame rate. This runs entirely client-side to choose a quality level (Low to Ultra); it is not sent to us and is not used to identify you.

Analytics, tag management & cookie consent

We use Google Tag Manager to load analytics tags (such as Google Analytics) on this site. In line with Google's Consent Mode, no analytics or advertising cookie is set until you make a choice in the cookie banner shown on your first visit:

  • Essential Only — only the strictly necessary local storage above is used; analytics stays off.
  • Accept All — analytics storage is enabled so we can see aggregate traffic and usage patterns (e.g. which battlegrounds or armies are popular). We do not currently run personalized advertising on this site; if that changes, this policy will be updated first and the same consent choice will govern it.

You can change your choice at any time from the cookie banner, or by clearing kg.cookie_consent from your browser's local storage to see the banner again.

Your rights (GDPR / CCPA)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK, or California, you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of personal data, and to withdraw analytics consent at any time. Because we hold no server-side account or profile tied to you, the fastest way to exercise these rights is locally: clearing your browser's cookies and local storage for this site removes every preference and consent choice described above. California residents may also submit a "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" request using the same method or by contacting us below; we do not sell personal information.

Children's privacy

War Chess 3D is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Because the game requires no account or personal details to play, no such information is knowingly gathered from any player.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the game evolves — for example, if we add a new analytics or advertising provider. The "Effective date" above will always reflect the latest revision; material changes affecting analytics or advertising will also be reflected in the cookie banner's consent options.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to info@warchess3d.com.
